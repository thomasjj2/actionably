Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Actionably helps you turn posts you have saved into things you actually do. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, so we kept it plain.

The short version
  • You sign in with Google. We keep your saves, plans and progress in your account so they sync across your devices.
  • To read a screenshot or caption, we send it to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) and get back a title, summary and actions.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads.
  • You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time, under My Account.

Who we are

"Actionably", "we" and "us" mean the people who run the Actionably app. You can reach us at actionably.us@outlook.com.

Actionably is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram, TikTok or Meta. The app does not connect to your Instagram account and cannot see your saved posts. You choose what to add, by uploading screenshots or pasting text.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication gives us your email address and a unique account ID. Google may also share basic profile details such as your name and profile picture with the sign in service. The app only displays your email address.

Content you add. This includes caption text you paste, screenshots you upload, an optional link to the original post, and any edits you make to a save.

Information the app creates for you. After reading a save, the app stores a category, title, summary, a list of actions with estimated minutes, and ingredients for recipes. It also stores your weekly plans, completed actions, weekly summaries, goals, progress counts, and the settings you chose in setup: your focus areas, weekly hours, and whether you prefer small daily steps or one weekly push.

Action records. For each planned action, we store its title, the day it was planned for, the outcome you gave it (done, partly done, skipped on purpose, moved to next week, or missed), when that happened, and any short note you choose to add. These records are kept in your account separately from your main data and are used to show your consistency over time. Please do not put sensitive personal details in notes.

Usage and limit data. To prevent abuse and control costs, we keep a daily count of how many AI requests and tokens each account uses, broken down by type of request (for example reading a save or setting up a goal), linked to your account ID. We also keep your plan status (free or paid) and whether you have used your free starter week. None of this contains the content of your saves. Our hosting provider also receives standard technical information with every request, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request.

Information stored on your device. The app keeps a copy of your data in your browser's local storage so it loads quickly and works when your connection is poor. Your browser also stores your sign in session. If you share a post to Actionably from another app, the shared images and text are held briefly in your browser's cache and removed once they are loaded into the Add a save form.

How we use information

  • To read your saves and turn them into actions, plans and goals.
  • To store your data in your account and sync it across your devices.
  • To show your weekly progress and consistency graph, which are worked out from your own records.
  • To keep the service secure, enforce usage limits, and fix problems.
  • To look at overall totals, such as how many goals were created in each category, so we can improve the app. We look at counts, not at what you wrote.
  • To respond when you contact us.

We do not use your content for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

How AI processing works

When you add a save, the app shrinks your screenshots in your browser and sends them, along with any text you entered, to our server function. That function checks that you are signed in, then forwards the content to Anthropic's Claude API to extract the title, summary and actions. When you build a weekly plan or ask for goal suggestions, the app sends your setup choices and a summary of your recent saves (titles, themes, summaries and actions) the same way.

We do not store your screenshot files in your account. The app keeps only the text results and a count of how many slides were read. According to Anthropic's published terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its models by default, and Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. Please read Anthropic's privacy policy for details.

Cookies and similar technologies

Actionably does not use advertising cookies. The app does not currently include analytics or advertising tools. It uses browser storage for essential things only: keeping you signed in, keeping a local copy of your data, and holding shared items until they are loaded. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep information

We keep your account data until you delete it. In the app, open My Account and choose Delete my account and data. This removes the document that holds your saves, plans and progress, your action records, your daily usage counts, your plan status, and it removes your sign in account. Google may keep backup copies for a limited time under its own policies.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database rules allow each signed in user to read and write only their own data, and our server function checks your sign in before it processes a request. No online service can promise perfect security, so please avoid adding highly sensitive information such as passwords, financial account numbers or health records to your saves.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view, edit and remove any save inside the app.
  • You can reset your progress inside the app, under My Account. This deletes your weekly history, completed actions, streak and action records, and keeps your saves and settings.
  • You can delete your account and data inside the app, under My Account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of anything the app cannot remove, by emailing actionably.us@outlook.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have additional rights over your personal information. Contact us to use them.

Content you add

Screenshots and captions may include other people's work or personal details. Please add only content you are comfortable sending to the AI service described above, and respect the rights of the people who created it.

Children

Actionably is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

International use

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those places.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and record the change in the version history below. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
